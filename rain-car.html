📐 Physics equations used in this model

1. Rain horizontal velocity vrain,x = − vrain · tan(θ)
θ is the angle from vertical. Positive θ (headwind) gives negative x (against car).
2. Relative velocity magnitude |vrel| = √( (vcar − vrain,x)² + vrain² )
Combines horizontal and vertical components of rain relative to the car.
3. Travel time t = d / vcar
Time to cover the distance d. If vcar = 0, we set t = 0.
4. Swept volume V = Afrontal · |vrel| · t
Afrontal = 2.2 m² (typical sedan).
5. Number of drops N = ρrain · V
ρrain is the drop density in drops per m³.
6. Mass of rain captured m = N · mdrop
mdrop = 1 × 10⁻⁶ kg (1 mg per drop).
Model assumptions: drops are tiny (1 mg each), car frontal area ≈ 2.2 m² (approx. sedan).
Only vertical & horizontal rain components relative to car are considered.
